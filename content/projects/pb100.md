*Foil Design, Flight Stability, Performance Prediction, Sail Development*

<!--more-->

Part of the **Juan Yacht Design** team during the development and optimization of the **Palm Beach XI** foiling yacht.

I played a central role in the **performance optimization study**, contributing to the definition and refinement of the yacht's hydrofoil package and overall sailing performance.

A major focus of my work was the **foil and elevator design**, including the definition of the **operating envelope**, sizing of the lifting surfaces, and development of foil sections considering **cavitation limits** across the expected speed range.

To improve flight stability, I conducted **CFD-FSI analyses** of the foil structures, exploring the influence of **composite laminate orientations** on structural deformation and the resulting hydrodynamic behaviour. This approach allowed the passive tuning of foil responses to enhance stability and control.

I also carried out **VPP simulations**, including analyses of **flight stability and dynamic performance**, supporting design decisions throughout the optimization process.

In parallel, I collaborated with the **sail design team**, providing performance assessments and VPP-based insights to support sail development and integration with the foiling platform.
