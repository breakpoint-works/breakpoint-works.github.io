*Foil Design, Flight Stability, Performance Prediction, Sail Development*

<!--more-->

Part of the [Juan Yacht Design Team](https://www.juanyachtdesign.com/) during the development and optimization of **Palm Beach XI**.

Central role in the **performance optimization study**, contributing to the definition and refinement of the yacht's hydrofoil package and overall sailing performance.

Key focus on **foil and elevator design**, including the definition of the **operating envelope**, sizing of the lifting surfaces, and development of foil sections considering **cavitation limits** across the expected speed range.

For flight stability, the influence of **composite laminate orientations** on structural deformation and the resulting hydrodynamic behaviour was investigated. This approach enabled passive tuning of foil response to improve stability and control.

**VPP simulations** were carried out, including analyses of **flight stability and dynamic performance**, supporting design decisions throughout the optimization process.

Close collaboration with the **sail design team** supported sail development through performance assessment and VPP-based insights, ensuring integration with the foiling platform.
